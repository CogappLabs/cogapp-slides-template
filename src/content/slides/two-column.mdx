## Two columns

<TwoCol>
  <div>
    ### Left

    - An even split needs no ratio.
    - Stacks to one column on narrow screens.
  </div>
  <div>
    ### Right

    - Mix headings, bullets and prose freely.
    - Each column is just MDX.
  </div>
</TwoCol>

### Weighted

<TwoCol ratio="2-1">
  <div>
    - `ratio="2-1"` gives this side twice the width. `"1-2"` flips it.
    - Also stops wide content (code, tables) blowing out a column.
  </div>
  <div>
    - Narrower support column.
  </div>
</TwoCol>
