notes: |
  `Video` takes a YouTube id or any share URL and sizes itself 16:9. It embeds
  through youtube-nocookie.com and lazy-loads, so nothing is requested until
  the slide is reached.

  Give it a real `title`: that is the iframe's accessible name, and a screen
  reader reads it in place of the video.

  Playback needs a network connection, so have a screenshot to fall back on if
  the room's wifi is unreliable.
