notes: |
  TwoCol is the component form of a two-column grid. Reach for it over a raw
  Tailwind grid when you want a weighted split: `ratio="2-1"` or `"1-2"`.
  It also guards against wide content (code, tables, long URLs) blowing a
  column past its share.
---

import TwoCol from "../../components/TwoCol.astro";
import Bullets from "../../components/Bullets.astro";

## Weighted columns

<TwoCol ratio="2-1">
  <div>
    ### Main
    <Bullets>
    - `ratio="2-1"` gives this side twice the width.
    - Use it when one column carries most of the content.
    </Bullets>
  </div>
  <div>
    ### Aside
    <Bullets>
    - Narrower support column.
    - `"1-2"` flips the weighting.
    </Bullets>
  </div>
</TwoCol>
